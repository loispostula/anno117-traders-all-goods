The NPC traders sell every finished good of Latium, Albion and Aegyptus: the 50 goods your residents consume, including the Prophecies of Ash and Dawn of the Delta goods. No raw goods, intermediates or building materials are added.

What changes

  • 9 traders: Diana and Valeria (Latium), Procurator Corvinus and Manx (Albion), Heron and Valeria (Aegyptus), and the pirates Caeso, Voada and Kandis.
  • Each added good refills 0.5 per minute up to 30. Goods a trader already sold keep their vanilla stock and refill rate. Prices are unchanged.
  • Statuettes and Latrunculi Sets (Prophecies of Ash) can now be sold by NPCs. Obsidian stays unsellable.
  • With Celtic Independence Overhaul active, Wicker Baskets and Ornamented Ponies are sold too. Without it, nothing changes.

Good to know

  • The game only shows goods you have already unlocked, so the list grows as you progress.
  • A trader never sells the goods it asks for (its "Demand" row).
  • Works with existing saves and can be removed safely.
  • Compatible with Taludas' Increased Trader Production and Increased Trader Storage.
  • Incompatible with Trading goods all and The sellers have all the building materials (same trader lists).

Feedback wanted

Tested in game on Latium; Albion and Aegyptus feedback from players further into the game is very welcome, especially whether goods unlocked in Latium show up at Albion traders. Report in the comments or on GitHub.
